QUESTIONS

Before you buy

Which harmonica do I need?

A 10-hole diatonic in the key of C. That is the standard beginner harp and the one this guide is built on, note for note. The key is stamped on the cover plate. Any brand works — Hohner, Suzuki, Lee Oskar, Fender.

I cannot read music. Is that a problem?

Not at all. There is no sheet music in this guide. Everything is hole numbers: a plain number means blow, a number with a minus means draw. You learn the whole notation in about ten seconds.

I have a chromatic harmonica. Does it work?

No. This is for the 10-hole diatonic — the small one used in blues, folk, country and rock. A chromatic has a slide button on the side and a different layout.

I have a harp in a different key, like G or A.

The hole numbers and every pattern still apply — only the note names change. If you are buying your first harp, get a C.

My close-up eyesight is not what it used to be.

The PDF is set in large type on a cream background with no light grey text, following the standard legibility guidance for readers over 60. You can print it or zoom in on your phone.
