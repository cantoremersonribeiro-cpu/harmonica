QUESTIONS

Before you buy

Which harmonica do I need?

A 10-hole diatonic harmonica in the key of C. That is the type this guide is built around. Any brand works — including Hohner, Suzuki, Lee Oskar and Fender.

I cannot read music. Is that a problem?

Not at all. There is no sheet music in this guide. Everything uses simple hole numbers: a plain number means blow, and a number with a minus means draw.

I have a chromatic harmonica. Does it work?

No. This guide is specifically for the 10-hole diatonic harmonica — the small harmonica commonly used for blues, folk, country and rock. A chromatic harmonica has a slide button and a different layout.

I have a harmonica in a different key, like G or A.

The hole numbers and patterns still apply. The note names will change with the key of the harmonica. If you are buying your first harmonica, a C harp is the one this guide uses.

Is this suitable if I am a complete beginner?

Yes. The guide starts with the basic layout of the instrument and does not assume that you can already read music or bend notes.

Can I print the guide?

Yes. The guide is designed to be read on your phone or printed and kept beside you while you practice.
